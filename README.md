# CPTS-360-CYA-2
Career Development Experience: Choose Your Own Adventure 02
VCEA Student Excellence Awards

This is an event being held by the Voiland College of Engineering and Architecture for celebrating the remarkable achievements of the WSU VCEA students. It is an event that recognizes and celebrates the sophomores, juniors, seniors, and grad students that show academic excellence, leadership, service, and meaningful contributions to the college community. It is a ceremony for student excellence of sophomores, juniors, seniors, and grad students that have made lasting important contributions to the college community.

The decision on the awards and recognition is chosen through a comprehensive college-level review process. The students show the dedication, innovation, and Cougar spirit that are seen as the best representation of defining VCEA. It is open to faculty, staff, students, alumni, and friends and pretty much anyone else that wants to experience the event and honor the accomplishments of these sophomores, juniors, seniors, and grad students.

I think attending this event aligns with my career goals in a couple ways because it allows me to honor peers and other students in the same or related majors being recognized for academic excellence in the college community. This allows me to see the potential oppotunities I could strive to achieve by the time I finish college and potentially network with these students to learn some tips for putting myself on a good path for these achievements. I could also meet with some faculty or staff that could provide some tips or guidance for internships in the future I can pursue.
